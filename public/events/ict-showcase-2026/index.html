Showcase CareerAnnual FREE

About this event

The ICT Showcase is the biggest student event of the year in the School of ICT. Students present real projects to industry guests, faculty, and peers — and get real feedback from the people who might one day be their colleagues.

This year's showcase featured 15 student projects, with awards for Business Acumen and Commercial Ready solutions. Students demonstrated working software, AI platforms, and data products solving real problems for real clients.
