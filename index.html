Your journey, step by step

Seven practical stages from arrival to your first ICT role in Tasmania. Take them in order — but revisit any step whenever you need.

  1. 1

    Start your life here

    Find accommodation (share house, homestay, or UTAS housing), set up your phone and bank account, learn Hobart transport, and stock groceries. Check your visa conditions (work hours, course attendance, OSHC) on VEVO and keep enrolment documents handy. Feeling overwhelmed is normal — settle in first, then build momentum.

    Arrival checklist
  2. 2

    Build your voice

    If you still need to pass your English test, make this your main focus. Join fixed local volunteering near UTAS to practise English in a safe, structured setting.

    Language centre + volunteering
  3. 3

    Join community

    Missed Open Day? Discover UTAS clubs, library services, and real student projects. Check your UTAS email and MyLO — important events and deadlines are posted there.

    Explore clubs & library
  4. 4

    Attend ICT events

    See what happened before and what is coming next — GDG Hobart, TasICT Festival, industry visits, CyberSecurity Melbourne, and more.

    ICT events list
  5. 5

    Open doors

    Open your first door into the industry you want to work in. Connect with tutors, alumni, event speakers, and local ICT people on LinkedIn.

    LinkedIn starter guide
  6. 6

    Build trust

    In Australia, references are often required before you can get a job. Build experience through UTAS Motorsport, Food Hub, Source Garden, Red Cross, and similar roles.

    Experience & references guide
  7. 7

    Grow into your future

    Apply, interview, and start your career in Tasmania. Then share your story so the next student believes it is possible.

    Job ready + Stories
