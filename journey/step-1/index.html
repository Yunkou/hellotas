Journey · Step 1

Start your life here

Get your survival basics sorted first. A stable foundation makes everything else possible.

Survival Income

Casual work

You need money to survive while you study, so start looking for casual work early.

Apply for a free Tax File Number (TFN) online via the ATO, keep a simple one-page resume, and check the UTAS Careers portal and job boards. Check your visa work-hour limit on VEVO first, and avoid cash-in-hand jobs below minimum wage — they can risk your visa.

Accommodation

Live

Secure your place early. UTAS housing fills up fast.

Apply for UTAS residences first, then use Flatmates or Facebook Marketplace to find a share house. Always inspect (in person or by video call) before paying anything — rental scams often target students booking from overseas.

The Daily Basics

Eat & Groceries

  • Coles / Woolworths supermarkets
  • UTAS Food Hub — free groceries
  • Asian grocers in North Hobart

Move & Transport

  • Metro Tasmania buses + real-time app
  • Greencard — student concession fares
  • Carry your student ID for concessions

Connect & Bank

  • Prepaid SIM — Telstra, Optus, Vodafone or budget brands like Amaysim
  • Open a CommBank / ANZ student account (you can start online)
  • Stay on prepaid until you have steady income

Safety First

  • Emergency: 000 (police, fire, ambulance)
  • UTAS SafeZone app — campus security
  • Non-urgent police assistance: 131 444
