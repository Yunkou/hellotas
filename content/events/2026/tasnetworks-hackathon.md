about:
  - "TasNetworks, the University of Tasmania's School of ICT and AWS ran a three-day hackathon at The Forest, the University's newly opened inner-city hub. It was officially launched on Monday 25 May 2026, with Madeleine Ogilvie, Minister for Innovation, Science and the Digital Economy, attending alongside representatives from all three partners."
  - "Fifty UTAS students worked on seven problem statements that came directly from TasNetworks staff through its internal Think Tank program, covering network data modelling, customer design processes, asset identification and protecting threatened birds. Teams built prototype solutions using cloud and emerging technology tools."
  - "A panel from TasNetworks, UTAS and AWS judged the teams on problem understanding, critical thinking, teamwork, architecture design, technical ambition, feasibility and how clearly they presented. The winning team was offered paid internships with TasNetworks plus prizes including Amazon vouchers from AWS, and the second-placed team won lunch with TasNetworks executives."
  - "Several of the challenge statements are expected to continue as School of ICT final year projects in Semester 2 2026 and Semester 1 2027."
