  - "AISA CyberCon Melbourne 2026, organised by the Australian Information Security Association (AISA), was held from 14 to 16 October 2026 at the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre (MCEC)."
  - "The three-day conference brought together cybersecurity professionals, industry leaders, researchers, and students to explore emerging cybersecurity threats, artificial intelligence, security technologies, and current industry challenges."
  - "Five University of Tasmania (UTAS) students were selected to attend the conference with sponsorship from the School of ICT, providing valuable opportunities to gain industry exposure beyond Tasmania. Special thanks to Dr David Herbert and Professor Nicole Herbert for their support."
  - "The conference featured keynote presentations, technical sessions, interactive activities, and professional networking opportunities. Highlights included a keynote by Jen Easterly, CEO of RSAC and former Director of CISA, along with sessions on cybersecurity education, building a cyber range on a student budget, and the CTRL+ALT+COMPETE cybersecurity game show."
  - "For UTAS students, the conference offered opportunities to connect academic learning with real-world cybersecurity practices, gain practical insights, engage with industry professionals, and explore potential career pathways."
